## Problem

A recruiter spends a few minutes on each portfolio, sometimes in English and sometimes in Spanish. I needed a site that loads instantly, reads in the visitor's language and never shows a broken link or a half-filled section.

## Contribution

- **Content as typed data.** Experience, education and certifications live in YAML with both languages, and projects in MDX. Velite validates them with Zod schemas at build time: a missing translation, `alt` text or image, or an invalid URL fails the build with the exact file and field.
- **Truly bilingual.** The home page picks the browser's language, remembers a manual choice, and the language switcher keeps the section you are reading.
- **Static and fast.** Every page is prerendered and served from Vercel's CDN, with optimized images and animations that respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- **Blocking quality checks.** GitHub Actions runs lint, unit tests, Playwright end-to-end tests and Lighthouse on every PR; nothing reaches `main` if they fail.

## Result

- Mobile Lighthouse (median of 3 runs): **97** performance and **100** accessibility, best practices and SEO.
- More than 40 automated tests, including behavior without JavaScript and with reduced motion.
- No pending field reaches production: a dedicated CI job checks it.

## What I learned

1. **Build-time validation turns silent errors into loud ones.** A typo in a URL stops being a broken link in front of a recruiter and becomes a red build.
2. **A 404 does not always respond 404.** With Next.js 16 Cache Components, once the page has started streaming, `notFound()` leaves the status at 200. Status codes must be tested, not assumed.
3. **Passing locally proves nothing.** One test depended on generated content that already existed on my machine; on a clean CI checkout it failed. Since then I verify every change from scratch.
